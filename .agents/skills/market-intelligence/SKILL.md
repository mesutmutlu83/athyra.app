---
name: market-intelligence
description: Research market context, user needs, category patterns, standards, and external evidence to inform product decisions without replacing product judgment.
---

Use current, credible sources when external research is requested or materially useful.
Prefer primary/authoritative sources for factual claims.
Separate:
- observed facts;
- source claims;
- assumptions;
- implications;
- open questions.

Avoid unsupported market-size or adoption claims.
Do not turn a competitor pattern into a requirement automatically.
Return evidence that the Product Manager can use in a decision.
For a durable finding, use `knowledge-management`: provide source URL, publication/observation date, checked date, confidence, and the canonical document affected. Do not promote a market claim to a product decision.
