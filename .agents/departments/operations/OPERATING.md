# Operations Operating Rules and Process

These are agent operating instructions. Canonical product and system facts remain in [department documentation](../../../docs/07-operations/README.md); shared gates remain in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

## Rules

Production availability, security, cost, or deployment-topology changes use the human gate. The weekly scanner produces candidates only.

## Process

Validate CI/runtime and rollback; operate the pinned weekly scan with its runbook and status, then hand operational evidence to Release.
