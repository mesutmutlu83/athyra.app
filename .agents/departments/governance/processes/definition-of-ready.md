# Definition of Ready

A meaningful implementation is ready when:
- objective/problem is understood;
- scope and out-of-scope are explicit enough;
- target user/actor is known when relevant;
- acceptance criteria are testable;
- critical edge cases are known;
- required UX/architecture/data/security impacts are identified;
- applicable Human Decision Gates are resolved;
- dependencies/blockers are understood;
- the required specialist roles are identified.
