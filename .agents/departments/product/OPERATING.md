# Product Operating Rules and Process

These are agent operating instructions. Canonical product and system facts remain in [department documentation](../../../docs/01-product/README.md); shared gates remain in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

## Rules

PM owns product strategy and scope. PO acceptance and researcher evidence inform decisions; external evidence does not approve a feature or a target.

## Process

Intake the request, decide the route and human gate, write testable acceptance when needed, then reconcile strategy/outcome documents from approved evidence.
