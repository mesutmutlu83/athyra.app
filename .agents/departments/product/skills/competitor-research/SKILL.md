---
name: competitor-research
description: Compare competitor product capabilities, UX patterns, pricing/packaging, positioning, or technical claims using current evidence and a consistent comparison frame.
---

Define the comparison dimensions before collecting examples.
Use the same dimensions for every competitor.
Distinguish documented capabilities from marketing claims.
Record source date/version when behavior may change.
Highlight gaps in evidence.
Do not copy competitor UX/code blindly and do not treat popularity as proof of suitability.
For a durable finding, use `knowledge-management`: provide source URL, publication/observation date, checked date, confidence, and the canonical document affected. Revalidate changing claims before use.
