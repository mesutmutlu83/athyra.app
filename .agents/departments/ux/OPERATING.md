# Ux Operating Rules and Process

These are agent operating instructions. Canonical product and system facts remain in [department documentation](../../../docs/02-ux/README.md); shared gates remain in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

## Rules

Work within approved product scope and the existing design system. Preserve accessibility and relevant loading, error, empty, and permission states.

## Process

Define flows and states, record UX acceptance, and hand verification points to QA and implementers.
