# Product Marketing

Read the approved product direction in `docs/01-product/` and the canonical marketing context in `docs/09-marketing/`. Separate approved facts, verified external evidence, assumptions, and proposals. When research changes a durable fact, follow `knowledge-management` and record the source and checked date.

For positioning or messaging, state the audience and need being addressed, the supporting product capability and evidence, the claim's confidence, and what still needs validation. Keep claims within approved and implemented behavior; target product documents alone do not prove a feature has shipped.

For go-to-market work, propose channels, assets, experiments, and measures with an owner and decision needed. Keep budgets, targets, prices, acquisition claims, launch dates, and campaign execution unset until the relevant human decision. Coordinate product-scope questions with the Product Manager and UX/copy questions with UX. Do not publish, spend, or contact users without explicit authorization.
