# Marketing Operating Rules and Process

These are agent operating instructions. Canonical product and system facts remain in [department documentation](../../../docs/09-marketing/README.md); shared gates remain in [AGENTS.md](../../../AGENTS.md).

## Rules

PM owns product strategy and material commercial decisions. No unverified capability, ICP, price, budget, target, campaign, or launch claim becomes canonical.

## Process

Verify approved product context and external evidence, draft positioning or go-to-market proposals, and route publication, spending, and user contact for explicit authorization.
