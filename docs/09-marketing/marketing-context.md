# Marketing Context

Owner: Marketing Specialist. Status: initial context for research and proposals, not an approved launch plan. Review when approved product scope or verified audience evidence changes.

## Supported product context

The [product vision](../01-product/vision.md) describes an athlete and coach planning product. The [product strategy](../01-product/strategy.md) sets the current focus and boundaries; it is the source for intended value, not evidence that a capability has shipped. Confirm implemented features and permission constraints before making a public claim.

## Open evidence and decisions

An ideal customer profile, validated messaging, channel performance, pricing, budget, launch date, conversion baseline, numerical targets, and approved campaign are not established here. Research findings should state publisher, source, checked date, applicability, and uncertainty. The Product Manager owns decisions that alter product scope or commercial commitments. The Marketing Specialist may prepare proposals and reconcile verified facts in this file; external publication, spending, and user contact require explicit authorization.
