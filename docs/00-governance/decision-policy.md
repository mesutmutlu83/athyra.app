# Decision Policy

## Human decides
Material product scope, user-visible behavior, major architecture/technology/vendor choices, breaking public contracts, security/privacy/payment/compliance decisions, destructive data operations, and material reliability/cost trade-offs.

## Team decides
Internal implementation details inside approved requirements and architecture: naming, module decomposition, ordinary refactors, algorithms, test organization, non-breaking internal interfaces, and other reversible engineering choices.

## Record
Material technical decisions use ADRs. Material product decisions live under `docs/01-product/product-decisions/` or the feature folder.
