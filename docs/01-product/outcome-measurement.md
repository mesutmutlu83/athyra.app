# Product Outcome Measurement

Owner: Product Manager. Status: **proposed measurement framework**, not approved success targets or a launch claim. It supports the [product strategy](strategy.md), [roadmap](roadmap.md), and [FEAT-001 requirements](../features/FEAT-001-initial-release/requirements.md). Product Owner checks definitions against accepted scope. No application events, analytics pipeline, or verified user baseline exist yet; the [tech-stack inventory](../04-engineering/tech-stack.md) records that product code and manifests are absent.

## Proposed measures

| ID / decision use | Population and calculation | Planned evidence source | Baseline / target / prerequisite |
|---|---|---|---|
| OM-01 — First weekly plan approval; assess whether a new eligible athlete reaches the core planning outcome | Denominator: controlled-beta athletes who start plan setup with required permissions/consents. Numerator: those athletes who approve a first weekly plan within 14 days of that start. Report cohort size and exclusions beside the rate. | Future application-owned setup and versioned plan-approval records; no health payload in the measurement export. | Baseline **unknown**; target **unset**. Define the setup event, eligibility and consent transitions, and deletion handling before collection. |
| OM-02 — Repeat weekly planning; assess whether the planning outcome remains useful | Denominator: eligible controlled-beta athletes who approved a plan for planning week W and remained eligible at the start of W+1. Numerator: those who approve a plan for W+1. Report cohort size and missing weeks; do not treat an individual pause as failure. | Future application-owned versioned plan approvals and eligibility state. | Baseline **unknown**; target **unset**. Approve the planning-week boundary and eligibility rules before collection. |
| OM-03 — Reviewed adaptation; assess whether observed or reported activity leads to a considered next step | Denominator: adaptation proposals shown to an eligible athlete or authorized coach. Numerator: proposals explicitly reviewed with an accept, edit, or reject decision within seven days. Report decision mix and missing outcomes; a high acceptance rate alone is not proof of quality. | Future proposal/version and explicit review-decision records, separated from imported records and user reports. | Baseline **unknown**; target **unset**. Confirm that the approved workflow emits complete proposal and review events before collection. |
| OM-04 — Planning safety guardrail; detect unusable output rather than maximize activity | Generated plan proposals rejected by deterministic feasibility/policy validation divided by all generated proposals, alongside validated user/coach infeasibility reports per approved plan. Report each numerator and denominator separately. | Future validator outcomes and categorized feedback; no raw health values in the aggregate review. | Baseline **unknown**; target **unset**. Define stable reason codes, deduplication and review ownership before collection. |

These definitions are candidates for instrumentation design. They do not authorize new tracking, consent use, health-data disclosure, or a change to the approved product flow. The PM and Security/Privacy owner must review the eventual event schema and data minimization before implementation. A binding target, launch threshold, or change to user-visible behavior requires an approved [product decision](product-decisions/PD-001-bootstrap-decisions.md).

## Review rhythm and record

PM reviews strategy and measure definitions **quarterly**, in the first full week of January, April, July, and October; next review due **2026-10-05**. During a controlled beta with verified instrumentation, PM reviews the actual aggregate readings **monthly**, in the first product work cycle after the first Monday of each month, with Product Owner, QA, and relevant UX/Security input where a finding changes interpretation. PM checks due dates at product intake; this is a work-cycle trigger, not a separate timed job. At each review, record date, phase/cohort, source and data-quality limits, baseline/actual value or `unknown`, decision or open question, owner, and next check. If no usable data exists, record `no data` and its collection blocker. Do not turn a suggested metric into an approved goal by adding a number without a product decision.

Initial inventory, **2026-09-28**: the repository has no application code, event schema, or production/beta telemetry. All four baselines are unknown and all targets unset. Decision: retain proposed definitions and defer numerical goals until instrumentation and a representative controlled-beta cohort exist. Next check: **2026-10-05** for definition and collection readiness; monthly reading begins only after real beta data exists.
