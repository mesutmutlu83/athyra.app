# Product Strategy

Owner: Product Manager. Status: synthesis of approved product direction, not a new scope or launch decision. Review when vision, roadmap, market evidence, or a product decision changes.

## Direction

Sportapp targets adults training in one or more sports, with optional coach support. Its value is weekly planning and adaptation from permissioned records, goals, availability, and feedback. The product keeps source data, user/coach statements, and Sportapp analysis distinguishable. See the [vision](vision.md), [personas](personas.md), and [approved decisions](product-decisions/PD-001-bootstrap-decisions.md).

## Initial focus and sequence

The first product surfaces are the native iPhone athlete/coach app and private browser-based admin panel backed by shared services. The [roadmap](roadmap.md) sequences identity and consent, read-only history, catalog and entitlement foundations, planning, feedback/adaptation, coach/admin workflows, and release verification. Expansion beyond the approved first release requires a product decision; the roadmap does not prove delivery.

## Boundaries and validation

The [vision](vision.md) excludes measurement, live workout recording, device pairing, watch apps, and writes to external health stores. Provider capability, catalog maturity, privacy, billing, and launch claims require the verification and human gates in the [product decisions](product-decisions/PD-001-bootstrap-decisions.md) and applicable feature records. Market or competitor findings are candidates under [knowledge management](../00-governance/knowledge-management.md) until validated; they do not change scope by themselves.

Product strategy is a Product Management capability, not a standing Strategy department or agent. Marketing execution, such as campaigns, channels, creative, and acquisition operations, has no standing owner/agent in this repository. If it begins, assign ownership and canonical documentation through a product decision; strategy ownership alone does not create a marketing function.

PM reviews this strategy quarterly in the first full week of January, April, July, and October, and earlier when an approved product decision changes direction. The next review is due 2026-10-05. [Outcome measurement](outcome-measurement.md) defines proposed indicators and the evidence required before numerical goals can be approved; no baseline or target is currently established.
